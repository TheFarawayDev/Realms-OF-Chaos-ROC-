# Texture atlases

Generated by `tools/generate.py` from the textures the mod ships. Each sheet comes at its own pixel size and
again four times larger (`@4x`). `index.json` maps every texture name to its column and row.

| Atlas | Tiles | Tile size | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `items.png` | 115 | 16 x 16 | every item texture (first frame of animated ones) |
| `weapon-designs.png` | 21 | 16 x 16 | weapon designs drawn ahead for future mythologies |
| `blocks.png` | 776 | 16 x 16 | every block texture (first frame) |
| `skins.png` | 235 | 64 x 64 | the raw 64x64 skins of the gods (one for each phase of a trial) and the passage sentinels |
| `portraits.png` | 120 | 16 x 32 | the gods and sentinels as they look from the front |
| `phases.png` | 45 | 48 x 32 | every god in the three phases of its trial, side by side: itself, its aegis, its wrath |
| `items-animated.png` | 86 rows | 16 x 16 | every frame of every animated item, one item per row |
