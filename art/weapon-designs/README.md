# Weapon designs

Generated by `tools/generate.py`. `sheet.png` shows every weapon silhouette in three metals; the first rows are designs for mythologies that are not in the mod yet:

* `katana`
* `naginata`
* `kunai`
* `tessen`
* `battleaxe`
* `mjolnir`
* `khopesh`
* `claymore`
* `halberd`
* `mace`
* `crossbow`

The single PNGs are also shipped in the mod under `textures/item/design/`, ready for an item to point at.
