Features

Tower of Zeus

Fifteen floors from the mossy foothills, through marble halls and a cloud sanctum, to the open summit of Olympus.

Tower of Poseidon

From sunlit sandstone shallows down through prismarine halls and the black trench to the Throne of the Deep.

Tower of Hades

Past the Fields of Asphodel and the fires of Tartarus, through the Halls of the Dead, to the throne of the Underworld.

Living floors

Every floor has its own terrain - and it moves. Wound a god and the ground shifts under you: pits rise into daises, halls fold into terraces, then settle back.

Signature moves

Every god has a move of its own - Zeus calls down the Wrath of Olympus, Hermes windsteps behind you, Hades opens the Gates of Tartarus. Each is named on screen before it lands.

Fair fights

Big attacks are telegraphed, and the ground breaks under them. Enraged gods make the floor erupt under your feet. Beaten a god? Call a rematch.

Three phases

At 65% a god raises its aegis: nothing hurts it until the shield pylons round the floor are broken. At 30% comes its wrath - the floor is lost ring by ring, and strikes fall from above, each marked before it lands.

Gods that learn

A god measures your weapons, their enchantments and your armour, and rises to match. Shoot it and it turns shots aside and closes in; stay hand to hand and its guard lashes back. Each phase changes how it looks.

Scan, don't warp

Nothing teleports you in an instant. The compass scans for a minute or more - rough readings, a Clean Lock, a burst of static. Raise relay towers to steady it, and waygates to travel between after a five-second channel.

Passages to explore

Between two floors lie one to ten levels, each a maze with a name like 7-B2: sealed chambers, arenas, vaults, chime rooms, beam rooms, trembling floors, crusher and dart halls, rune chambers that flood. Every level is harder than the last.

Different in every world

Passages are generated from your world's seed - where the rooms connect, where the seals hide, what each room holds. No two worlds have the same towers.

Find your way

A chart with fog of war inks in each room as you walk into it, a bar counts the seals you have broken, and a guide points you to the nearest one left. The next level is built while you explore.

A story to uncover

Chaos is unmaking the world, and the gods who hid from it have forgotten why. The Oracle tells it in nineteen chapters, written as you climb - and every god you beat is not slain, but woken.

Find the way in

The gates were broken on purpose. Follow your compass to a Ruined Gate in the Overworld, track down three keystones in shrines across the world, and wake it.

A living, growing hub

The Oracle's flame burns at its heart and echoes of the gods watch from their gates. Hand in what it asks for and the hub rises through five tiers - a sanctuary that mends you, a daily bounty board, faster waygates.

Mastery

Every god woken and every passage walked earns a point for the mastery tree: twelve skills across combat, survival and exploration, re-spendable at any time.

Climb together

Form a party of up to four and enter a tower on your highest member's floor. Progress stays your own - nobody climbs past a god they have not beaten - but friends can always come and help.

The tower adapts

More challengers make a harder trial, the world's difficulty sets its pace, a god that keeps beating you relents a little, and a passage seals itself again for anyone who has not walked it.

Sentinels

Sixty-three kinds, twenty-one to a tower. Shield-bearers shelter their allies, wardens take their wounds, singers raise the fallen - while bombers, snipers, thieves and heralds make trouble. Kill the helper first.

The Gods

One god per floor, each with its own signature move. Floor 1 is where you start; floor 15 is the summit.

In game

Menus and on-screen prompts, not chat spam

Codex of Chaos

Your whole journey in one menu: the three towers, every floor and god, what is defeated and what is next. Click a floor you have reached to travel there, go to the hub, or leave the realms.

Trial Altar

Each floor's altar opens its own screen: who waits on this floor, and one button that begins the trial - or, once they have fallen, sends you up.

Tower HUD

A bar at the top of the screen always shows which tower and floor you are on. Arrivals, trials and victories appear as titles; short notices sit above the hotbar.

What changed in 1.2

  • A tutorial: a handbook that opens for new players, and tips the first time things happen.
  • Real screens for the Codex, the altar, the story and the party.
  • Passages have two or three levels each; 879 rooms to a tower.
  • The floor erupts in a fight, and every heavy move breaks the ground.
  • Parties of up to four, with each player's progress and loot their own.
  • Trials scale with the group; passages have twelve kinds of sentinel.
  • The realms' walls can no longer be broken.
  • A story, told by the Oracle in nineteen chapters.
  • The realms must be found: a Ruined Gate in the Overworld, woken with three keystones.
  • A livelier hub: the Oracle, echoes of the gods, pilgrims, a canal and gardens.
  • Each god line is now a 15-floor tower you climb one god at a time.
  • Between every two floors lies a passage to explore: a maze generated from your world's seed, with seals to break, guardians, treasure and a shrine.
  • A passage map that fills in as you explore, and a compass that points the way.
  • Every floor has its own terrain and materials, and reshapes itself mid-fight.
  • Rooms have columns, ribbed ceilings, hanging lights and themed props.
  • Every god has a named signature move, lunges or hops away, and enrages at half health.
  • Some gods keep their distance and fight at range.
  • Fallen gods can be called back for a rematch.
  • A bigger Codex that draws each tower floor by floor.
  • The hub has an exit gate; the Codex and /roc leave take you out too.
  • Chat messages are gone: menus, titles and a tower bar instead.
  • Builds are labelled Alpha or Beta until they are battle-tested.

Install

Steps

  1. Install Java 25 and your loader for Minecraft 26.3.
  2. Fabric only: add Fabric API.
  3. Put the downloaded .jar in your mods folder.
  4. Join a world: you are given the Codex, the Compass of Chaos and the Oracle's letter. Follow the compass to a Ruined Gate.
