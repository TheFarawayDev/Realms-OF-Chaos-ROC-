Install

  • Install Java 25 and your loader for Minecraft 26.3.
  • Drop the .jar into your mods folder.
  • Right-click the Compass of Chaos or run /roc hub to reach the Hub of Realms.

What's inside

  • A hub with three gates to the Olympian Heights, the Abyssal Palace and the Underworld.
  • Ranked god lines, from the starters Iris, Galatea and Charon up to Zeus, Poseidon and Hades.
  • Every god drops a relic weapon with its own power and a right-click Divine Surge.
