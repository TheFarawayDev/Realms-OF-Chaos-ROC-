Features

Zeus

Climb from Iris to the King of the Gods across the Olympian Heights. Lightning, wisdom and war.

Poseidon

From Galatea to the Lord of the Seas in the Abyssal Palace. Tidal waves and crushing currents.

Hades

From Charon to the Lord of the Underworld. Drain life, wither flesh, command the dead.

Hub of Realms

One hub, three gates. Choose any path; every line is independent and saved per player.

Relic weapons

Every god drops a weapon scaled by rank with its own hit power and a right-click Divine Surge.

Fabric and Forge

One codebase, two loaders, same content and behaviour on both.

Install

Steps

  1. Install Java 25 and your loader for Minecraft 26.3.
  2. Fabric only: add Fabric API.
  3. Put the downloaded .jar in your mods folder.
  4. In game, right-click the Compass of Chaos or run /roc hub.
