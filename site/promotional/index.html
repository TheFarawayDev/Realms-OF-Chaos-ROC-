New in v1.2 Alpha

Climb the towers

Every god line is now a fifteen-floor tower. One god per floor. Beat the god, the Gate of Ascent opens, and up you go.

Floors that move

Wound a god and the arena reshapes itself mid-fight. Pits rise into daises. Halls fold into terraces. Then it all settles back.

Forty-five unique floors

Ten terrain layouts across four material tiers per tower, from the mossy Foothills of Olympus to the Throne of the Underworld.

Rooms worth standing in

Columns between the windows, ribbed ceilings with hanging lights, and props that fit each tier: azaleas, sea lanterns, braziers of soul fire.

Real in-game menus

The Codex of Chaos draws each tower floor by floor. The Trial Altar has its own screen. A tower bar tracks your climb. No chat spam.

Not just swords

Nine kinds of weapon, from daggers and scythes to bows, staffs and thrown thunderbolts. Gods with ranged weapons fight at range.

Level, forge, ascend

Relics level up with every kill, and the Forge of Chaos hammers up to five stars onto them with materials taken from the gods of every tier of the tower.

Tarot and voodoo

Twenty-two tarot cards from Hades' tower, a Deck of Fate that never runs out, and voodoo dolls that hurt what they are bound to.

Fabric and Forge

One mod, both loaders, Minecraft 26.3. Every god drops an animated relic weapon with its own power.

Short post 

Realms of Chaos v1.2 Alpha is out.

Climb three fifteen-floor towers, one god per floor, from Iris to Zeus, Galatea to Poseidon and Charon to Hades. Every floor is its own arena, and it reshapes itself mid-fight. Every god drops a relic weapon.

Fabric and Forge, Minecraft 26.3. By TheFarawayDev.

Feature list 

Realms of Chaos v1.2 Alpha
- 3 towers of 15 floors: Zeus, Poseidon, Hades
- 45 gods, one per floor, each with its own look
- 45 animated relic weapons in 9 kinds: blades, daggers, polearms, heavy weapons, scythes, staffs, bows, thrown weapons, relics
- Ranged gods, weapon levels 1-10 and five forged stars at the Forge of Chaos
- 22 tarot cards and the Deck of Fate from Hades' tower, plus craftable voodoo dolls
- Living floors that shift shape during a trial
- 45 different floors: 10 terrain layouts across 4 material tiers per tower
- In-game menus: the Codex of Chaos, the Trial Altar, a tower progress bar
- A Realms of Chaos advancements tab
- Fabric and Forge, Minecraft 26.3
This is an Alpha: expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks.
